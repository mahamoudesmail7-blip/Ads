Data Deletion Instructions

AI Media Buyer — operated by Business Ai. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Remove the app's access

  1. In AI Media Buyer, open the Meta connection settings and choose Disconnect. The stored access token is removed immediately.
  2. In Facebook, open Settings → Business integrations, select the app and remove it.

Ask us to delete your data

Send an email to mahamoudesmail7@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, including your name and the Meta account or ad account ID concerned.

We confirm your request and delete the data received from your Meta account within 30 days, except records we are legally required to keep.
