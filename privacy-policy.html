Privacy Policy — AI Media Buyer

Operated by Business Ai. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

AI Media Buyer is an internal business tool used by our own team to manage our own advertising on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and to follow the orders of our online stores. It is not offered to the general public.

Data we receive from Meta

When an authorised team member connects a Meta account, we receive, only for the assets that member has access to:

  • The connected user's Meta ID and name.
  • An access token, which is stored encrypted.
  • The list of businesses, ad accounts, Facebook Pages and linked Instagram accounts.
  • Campaign, ad set, ad and creative settings, and their performance metrics (for example spend, results, cost per result).
  • Ad account billing information needed for monitoring, such as the available balance and spending limits.

We do not receive or store Meta passwords, payment card numbers, or private messages.

How we use it

  • To show our team the performance of our ads in dashboards and reports.
  • To create, duplicate, schedule, pause or adjust our own campaigns when a team member asks for it or switches on an automation.
  • To send our team alerts, for example when an account balance is low or an ad is rejected.

Some summaries of advertising performance may be processed by an AI service provider to produce analysis for our team. We do not sell Meta data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles of individuals or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Store and order data

The tool also processes order information from our own online stores (such as customer name, phone number, address and ordered products) to confirm and follow up orders. It is used only for running our business.

Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a managed database and application hosting used by our service. Access requires a personal login and is limited by role. Our hosting, database and AI providers process data only to run the service. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep advertising data while it is needed for reporting. Disconnecting the Meta account removes the stored access token immediately. You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you or received from your Meta account; see Data deletion instructions.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw the app's access from your Facebook settings (Settings → Business integrations).
